# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two distinct audiences on one app: Nepali buyers browsing and purchasing everyday goods (groceries, fashion, electronics, home goods) across categories and vendors, and Nepali sellers running their own storefront within the marketplace (managing products, pricing/discounts, orders, and fulfillment). Buyers are price- and deal-conscious, often on mobile, comparing across sellers. Sellers are small/independent shop owners who need a fast, low-friction dashboard, not an enterprise back office.

## Product Purpose

AaganBazaar is a Nepal-focused, multi-vendor ecommerce marketplace connecting local sellers directly with local buyers, with lower platform fees than large international marketplaces. Success looks like buyers finding and trusting a purchase decision quickly, and sellers managing their storefront without friction.

## Brand Personality

Bold and energetic - a lively bazaar, not a sterile storefront. Deal-driven and vibrant (flash deals, category sellouts, visible discounts) while staying legible and uncluttered - energy comes from color and motion, not from cramming every pixel with UI like typical high-density Chinese marketplace apps (AliExpress, Daraz, Temu).

## Anti-references

Generic cluttered marketplace chrome: dense multi-row navbars, tiny crowded icon clusters, gray-on-gray utility bars, ad-hoc mixed alignment. Avoid the "everything crammed above the fold" density of AliExpress/Daraz/Temu even when borrowing structural ideas from them.

## Design Principles

- Energy through color and hierarchy, not density - bold orange accents and confident type do the work that clutter does elsewhere.
- One primary action per view - the navbar guides toward search and cart, not a wall of equally-weighted icons.
- Trust is a visual language - local/community framing (Nepali script accents, "local seller" cues) reinforced consistently, not just in copy.
- Mobile-considered by default - buyers are often on phones; navbar and controls must hold up at narrow widths, not just desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements stated beyond general good practice: target WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text/icons), full keyboard operability for nav/search/cart, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any interactive/entrance animation.
